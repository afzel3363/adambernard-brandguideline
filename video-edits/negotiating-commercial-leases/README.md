# Negotiating Commercial Leases: podcast edit

Final edit of `negotiating commercial lease.mp4` (Mohammed, Property Team).

- **File:** `AdamBernard_Negotiating_Commercial_Leases_EDIT.mp4` (stored with Git LFS)
- **Format:** 1920x1080, 30 fps, H.264 + AAC 192k, 8:34
- **Palette:** off-white `#F5F0E8`, near-black `#0B0B0B`, red `#E0252B` (highlights only), grey `#6B6B6B`
- **Type:** Montserrat

## What was added

- Dark question cards for the 8 audience questions (Question 01 to 08), built word by word in sync with the speech.
- Light and dark full-screen cards for key information: title, the "10 years / £40 invoice" hook, 3 individual shops, liability cap, Landlord and Tenant Act 1954, recap and end card.
- Right-side chips and checklist panels for legal terms (rent schedule, heads of terms, break clause risks, personal guarantees, service charges).
- Word-by-word captions with key terms on a red highlight box.
- Punch-in reframes every 5 to 10 seconds.
- Subtle SFX: whooshes, clicks, pops and a soft low hit on dark cards, mixed well under the voice.
- Two cuts, each hidden under a full-screen card:
  - 7:07: removed the sentence about serving a "section 42 notice for a lease extension" under the 1954 Act. Section 42 notices belong to residential flat lease extensions (Leasehold Reform, Housing and Urban Development Act 1993); commercial renewals use section 25 notices or section 26 requests.
  - 7:37: removed a verbal stumble ("commercial leave, it's been a").

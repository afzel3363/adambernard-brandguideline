A fee waiver is
a request. Not an
application.

You ask the Home Office to waive the extension fee because you can't afford it. If they agree, you then submit your FLR application.

Done right, the date you asked for the waiver counts as your application date. That's what protects you if your visa runs out while you wait.

same route only

Your follow-up application must be on the same route you named in your fee waiver request.

Asked for a waiver on the partner route? Your extension must be on the partner route too.

Switch routes (e.g. to private life) and your application date becomes the day you actually submit.

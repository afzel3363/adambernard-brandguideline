## Check before posting

These are in the recorded audio, not the graphics:

1. **7:15** Section 42 notice "for a lease extension" under the 1954 Act is incorrect. Section 42 notices are for residential flat lease extensions (Leasehold Reform, Housing and Urban Development Act 1993). Commercial renewals under the Landlord and Tenant Act 1954 use section 25 notices (landlord) or section 26 requests (tenant). Recommend cutting this sentence.
2. **0:27** "between the buyer and the seller": a lease is negotiated between landlord and tenant.
3. **4:19** Personal guarantees are framed as given by "directors and landlords". In practice they are given by directors of the tenant company.
4. Graphics use "Adam Bernard Solicitors"; the speaker says "Adam Bernard's Solicitors". Confirm the preferred firm name.

## Slide copy

1. **Hook:** Your fee waiver used to protect your date. From 8 October, only if you do this.
2. **Basics:** A fee waiver is a request. Not an application. You ask the Home Office to waive the fee, then submit your actual FLR application. Done right, the waiver date counts as your application date.
3. **The change:** Same route. No switching. Your follow-up application must be on the same route as your fee waiver request. Switch routes and your application date becomes the day you submit.
4. **The clock:** 10 working days from receiving the decision. Weekends and bank holidays don't count. A paid, same-route application made before the decision also keeps the waiver date.
5. **The risk:** Your protected date disappears. Worked example timeline. The gap can leave you without lawful status.

## Caption (Instagram / Facebook)

Applying for a fee waiver on your spouse or partner visa extension? Read this before 8 October.

A fee waiver is a request, not an application. Get the follow-up right and the date you asked for the waiver counts as your application date. That's what protects you if your visa expires while you wait.

From 8 October 2026 (HC 584), that protection only holds if:
✔️ your follow-up application is on the same route as your fee waiver request, and
✔️ you submit it within 10 working days of receiving the decision.

Miss either one and your application date becomes the day you actually submit. If your visa has already run out by then, that gap matters.

## Caption (LinkedIn)

A quiet change in HC 584 lands on 8 October 2026 and it will catch people close to visa expiry.

Paragraph 34G now ties the protected application date from a fee waiver request to two conditions: the follow-up application must be on the same route as the waiver request, and it must be submitted within 10 working days of receiving the decision (or paid for and submitted before the decision arrives).

Apply on a different route, or submit late, and the application date is the day you submit. For partner and private-life applicants whose leave expires while the waiver is pending, that can mean a gap in lawful status.
